Privacy Policy

tl;dr ("the Service") respects your privacy. The Service includes our browser extension (for any browser) and Gmail add-on (for any email client). This policy explains how the Service collects, uses, and protects your information.


Information We Collect

The Service only processes the text content that you select or that is visible on the active web page or open email message to generate summaries using an AI service (Llama AI via Groq API). The Service does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, including your name, email address, or browsing history.

User Data

The Service accesses Google user data only when you explicitly grant permission via OAuth. The Service may access Gmail email content to generate summaries of email threads or individual messages you select.

Data is used exclusively to provide the text summarization you request. It is never used for training AI models, advertising, marketing, analytics, or profiling. Data is never sold or shared with third parties except as needed to provide the summary (Groq API, which also does not retain or train on your data).

How We Use Your Data

Selected or visible text is sent securely to a Groq API endpoint for AI summarization. No data is stored permanently on our servers. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties.

Data Security & Protection

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data. We use encryption to protect your information.

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS) for all data between your device, the Service, and Groq API.
  • No local storage: The Service does not store sensitive data on your device beyond the active session.
  • Zero Data Retention (ZDR): Groq API data collection is disabled; inputs and outputs are not logged or stored.
  • Minimal data exposure: Only the text you select is transmitted - never your full inbox, browsing history, or credentials.
  • No third-party access: Only Groq API receives your data, solely to generate the summary.

Permissions

The Service requires permissions to access visible content in your active browser tab or open email message, and to communicate with the Groq API. For Gmail, the Service requests read-only access to email content you select - never your contacts, senders list, or account settings. The Service cannot access any Google user data without your explicit consent via the OAuth screen.

Data Retention & Deletion

The Service does not retain any user data. All text sent for summarization is processed in real time and permanently discarded immediately after the summary is generated. There is no database, cache, or log that stores your data at any point. Since no data is stored, there is nothing to delete - but if you ever want to revoke Google access, you can do so from your Google Account security settings.

Your Consent

By using the tl;dr Service, you consent to the processing of your selected text content as described in this Privacy Policy.
