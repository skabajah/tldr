Privacy Policy

tl;dr ("the Service") includes our browser extension and Gmail add-on. This policy explains what data the Service processes and how.


Information We Collect

The Service processes only the text you select or that is visible on the active page or open email message, to generate a summary. It does not collect or store personal data such as your name, email address, or browsing history.

User Data

The Service accesses Google user data only with your explicit permission via OAuth, and only reads the Gmail content you select. This data is used solely to generate the summary you request. It is never used for training, advertising, analytics, or profiling, and is never sold.

Third-Party Services

Two third-party services receive your text;

  • Google Apps Script: routes requests between the Service and the AI model.
  • Groq: hosts the AI models that generate the summary.

Data Security & Protection

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS) between your device, the Service, and the AI service.
  • No local storage: No sensitive data is stored on your device beyond the active session.
  • Zero Data Retention (ZDR): Groq API data collection is disabled; inputs and outputs are not logged or stored.
  • Minimal data exposure: Only the text you select is transmitted.

Permissions

The Service requires permission to read visible content in your active tab or open email and to communicate with the AI service. For Gmail, access is read-only and limited to the content you select.

Data Retention & Deletion

The Service retains no user data. Text is processed in real time and discarded immediately after the summary is generated. To revoke Google access, use your Google Account security settings.

Your Consent

By using the Service, you consent to the processing described in this policy.
